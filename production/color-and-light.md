# Color & Light

## Color Theory for Storytelling

### Color = Emotion
Color is the fastest way to make an audience feel something before a single word is spoken.

| Color | Emotion/Association | Example Use |
|-------|-------------------|-------------|
| Red | Passion, danger, anger, love, urgency | Schindler's List (red coat), American Beauty |
| Blue | Cold, isolation, calm, sadness, trust | Moonlight, Blade Runner 2049 |
| Yellow | Joy, warmth, caution, madness | Amélie, Breaking Bad |
| Green | Nature, sickness, envy, growth, alien | The Matrix (green tint = digital), Vertigo |
| Orange | Warmth, energy, autumn, comfort | Blade Runner, any golden hour |
| Purple | Royalty, mysticism, unease, fantasy | Black Panther, A Clockwork Orange |
| White | Purity, sterility, emptiness, death | 2001: A Space Odyssey, Ex Machina |
| Black | Power, mystery, death, elegance | Film noir, The Dark Knight |

### Color Palettes as Storytelling

**Monochromatic:** One color family. Unity, obsession, trapped in one emotional state.
**Complementary:** Opposite colors (blue/orange). Tension, contrast, visual pop. Hollywood's favorite.
**Analogous:** Adjacent colors. Harmony, cohesion, natural feel.
**Triadic:** Three evenly spaced. Vibrant, playful, Wes Anderson territory.
**Desaturated:** Low saturation. Grit, realism, depression, period feel.
**Hyper-saturated:** Cranked color. Fantasy, fever dream, pop art, music video.

### Color Shifts in Narrative
Color should evolve with the story:
- Happy → sad: warm palette gradually cooling
- Innocence → corruption: saturated → desaturated, or clean → sickly green
- Day → night (literal or metaphorical): shifting palette signals emotional change
- The Storycrafter emotional map should directly inform color progression

### Describing Color for AI Prompts
- Be specific: "teal and amber" not "blue and orange"
- Reference films: "color palette like Blade Runner 2049"
- Use color temperature: "warm tungsten" vs "cool daylight"
- Specify saturation: "muted earth tones" vs "vivid neon"
- Describe contrast: "high contrast chiaroscuro" vs "flat, even, overcast lighting"

## Lighting

### Key Lighting Setups

**Key Light:** Primary light source. Direction and quality define the mood.
- Front: flat, safe, commercial
- Side: dimension, drama, texture
- Back: silhouette, mystery, separation
- Below: horror, unnatural, interrogation
- Above: heavenly, natural, authority

**Three-Point Lighting:** Key + Fill + Back. The standard. Adjust ratios for mood:
- Equal key/fill = flat, commercial, safe
- Strong key, weak fill = dramatic, shadows, cinema
- No fill = noir, extreme drama

**Natural Light:** Sun, windows, practicals. Documentary feel, authenticity, golden hour magic.

### Named Lighting Styles

| Style | Description | Mood | Example |
|-------|------------|------|---------|
| **Rembrandt** | Key light at 45°, triangle of light on shadow cheek | Classical, dramatic | Godfather |
| **Split** | Light from direct side, half face lit, half dark | Duality, conflict | Breaking Bad |
| **Butterfly** | Light from directly above and front | Glamour, beauty | Classic Hollywood portraits |
| **Loop** | Key slightly to side, small shadow from nose | Natural, flattering | Standard portrait work |
| **Rim/Edge** | Light from behind, outlining subject | Separation, ethereal, sci-fi | Blade Runner, Alien |
| **Practical** | Light sources visible in frame (lamps, screens, neon) | Realistic, atmospheric | Collateral, Drive |
| **Chiaroscuro** | Extreme contrast, deep shadows | Drama, power, noir | Caravaggio → film noir |
| **High Key** | Even, bright, minimal shadows | Happy, safe, commercial | Sitcoms, Apple ads |
| **Low Key** | Mostly shadow, selective highlights | Mystery, tension, horror | Noir, thriller |

### Lighting for AI Prompts
AI models respond well to:
- Named styles: "Rembrandt lighting," "chiaroscuro"
- Time of day: "golden hour," "blue hour," "harsh midday"
- Quality: "soft diffused light," "hard directional light"
- Source: "lit by neon signs," "single candle," "overcast window light"
- Contrast: "high contrast," "low contrast, flat lighting"
- Temperature: "warm candlelight," "cool fluorescent"
