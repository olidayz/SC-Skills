# Cinematography

## Shot Types

### By Size
- **Extreme Wide Shot (EWS):** Subject tiny in environment. Establishes scale, isolation, epic scope. "This world is bigger than you."
- **Wide Shot (WS):** Full body in environment. Context. Where are we, who is here.
- **Medium Wide (MWS):** Knees up. Action space. Good for movement, gesture.
- **Medium Shot (MS):** Waist up. The workhorse. Conversation, connection, neutral.
- **Medium Close-Up (MCU):** Chest up. Starting to get personal. Good for dialogue.
- **Close-Up (CU):** Face fills frame. Emotion, reaction, intimacy. This is where performance lives.
- **Extreme Close-Up (ECU):** Eyes, mouth, hands, an object detail. Intensity. Forces the audience to focus.
- **Insert/Detail:** An object or action close-up. Provides information or symbolism.

### By Angle
- **Eye Level:** Neutral. We see them as equals.
- **Low Angle:** Looking up at subject. Power, dominance, heroism, intimidation.
- **High Angle:** Looking down at subject. Vulnerability, weakness, diminishment.
- **Dutch Angle (tilted):** Unease, instability, something is wrong. Use sparingly.
- **Bird's Eye / Overhead:** Detachment, pattern, god's perspective. Good for showing spatial relationships.
- **Worm's Eye:** Extreme low. Dramatic, surreal, childlike perspective.

### By Position
- **Over the Shoulder (OTS):** Framed over one character's shoulder at another. Connection, conversation, point of view.
- **POV (Point of View):** We see exactly what the character sees. Immersion, empathy, horror.
- **Two-Shot:** Both characters in frame. Relationship, comparison, tension.
- **Single:** One character alone in frame. Isolation, focus, internal moment.

### When to Use What
- **Establishing scene:** Start wide, move closer
- **Building tension:** Gradually tighten framing
- **Emotional peak:** Close-up or ECU
- **Revelation:** Wide after a series of tights (context changes everything)
- **Power shift:** Change angle — character who was shot from below is now shot from above

## Camera Movement

### Types
- **Static:** Locked tripod. Stability, control, contemplation. Don't underestimate stillness.
- **Pan:** Horizontal rotation on axis. Reveals, follows action, surveys space.
- **Tilt:** Vertical rotation on axis. Reveals height, scale, or shifts attention up/down.
- **Dolly (track):** Camera moves toward/away from subject. Intimacy (dolly in) or distance (dolly out).
- **Truck:** Camera moves laterally. Follows walking subjects, reveals space.
- **Crane/Jib:** Vertical camera movement. Ascending = liberation, descending = enclosure.
- **Handheld:** Human imperfection. Energy, urgency, documentary feel, chaos.
- **Steadicam:** Smooth floating movement. Following, dreamlike, tracking through space.
- **Zoom:** Lens changes, camera stays. Compression effect. Push = focus/urgency, pull = reveal/isolation. (Different from dolly — zoom flattens, dolly preserves depth.)
- **Dolly Zoom (Vertigo effect):** Dolly one direction + zoom opposite. Spatial distortion. Dread, realization, disorientation.
- **Whip Pan:** Fast pan creating motion blur. Energy, transition, surprise.

### Movement = Emotion
- No movement = calm, control, dread (depending on context)
- Slow movement = contemplative, building
- Fast movement = energy, chaos, urgency
- Smooth = dream, grace, confidence
- Shaky = anxiety, realism, adrenaline

## Lens Language

### Wide Lens (14-35mm equivalent)
- **Look:** Expands space, distorts edges, deep focus
- **Feels like:** Epic, isolated, environmental, slightly surreal at extremes
- **Use for:** Establishing, landscapes, making spaces feel vast, close-ups with environmental context
- **AI prompt language:** "wide-angle lens," "expansive," "deep depth of field"

### Normal Lens (35-50mm)
- **Look:** Closest to human eye perception
- **Feels like:** Natural, neutral, documentary
- **Use for:** When you want the audience to feel present without manipulation
- **AI prompt language:** "natural perspective," "50mm lens," "eye-level"

### Telephoto (85-200mm+)
- **Look:** Compresses space, shallow depth of field, isolates subject
- **Feels like:** Intimate, voyeuristic, surveillance, dreamy bokeh
- **Use for:** Portraits, intimate moments, isolating subject from environment, making crowds feel dense
- **AI prompt language:** "shallow depth of field," "bokeh," "telephoto compression," "blurred background"

### Macro
- **Look:** Extreme close, reveals texture invisible to naked eye
- **Feels like:** Scientific, alien, meditative, obsessive
- **Use for:** Texture, detail, symbolic objects, ASMR-adjacent content
- **AI prompt language:** "macro photography," "extreme detail," "shallow focus"

### Anamorphic
- **Look:** Oval bokeh, horizontal lens flares, wider aspect ratio, slight edge distortion
- **Feels like:** Cinematic, epic, romantic
- **Use for:** Anything that needs to feel like a movie
- **AI prompt language:** "anamorphic lens," "oval bokeh," "horizontal lens flare," "cinemascope"
