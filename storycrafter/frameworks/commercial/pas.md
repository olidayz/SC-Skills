# PAS (Problem → Agitate → Solution)

## What It Is
Copywriting's sharpest framework. Identify the problem, twist the knife, then offer relief. It works because pain is a stronger motivator than pleasure. People will do more to escape discomfort than to gain comfort.

## The 3 Stages

1. **Problem** — Name the pain. Be specific. The audience should think "that's me."
   - "You spend 3 hours every week on invoicing."
   - Don't be vague. "Business is hard" = weak. "You're losing clients because your invoices arrive late" = strong.

2. **Agitate** — Make the pain worse. Show consequences. Project into the future. What happens if this problem continues?
   - "That's 150 hours a year. Almost a full month of your life. On paperwork."
   - "Meanwhile, your competitors are closing deals."
   - This is NOT manipulation — it's showing the real cost of inaction.

3. **Solution** — Offer the relief. Your product/service/idea is the answer.
   - "AutoInvoice sends perfect invoices in 10 seconds."
   - The solution should feel inevitable. The agitation made the need clear; the solution fills it.

## When to Use It
- **Best for:** Problem-aware audiences, B2B, SaaS, health/wellness, any product that solves a real pain
- **Tone fit:** Empathetic but urgent. Not fear-mongering — truth-telling.
- **Format fit:** 30-60s ads, email sequences, landing pages, sales pages
- **Not ideal for:** Luxury/aspiration brands, entertainment content, awareness campaigns

## Timing (30s ad)
- Problem: 3-5s
- Agitate: 10-15s
- Solution: 10-15s (includes CTA)

## Example
**A hypothetical SaaS ad:**
- Problem (3s): Founder staring at spreadsheet. Text: "Still doing bookkeeping manually?"
- Agitate (12s): Montage — late nights, errors, missed deadlines, partner frustrated. "Every mistake costs you. Every hour could be spent growing."
- Solution (15s): Product demo. Clean dashboard. "One click. Done." CTA: "Free trial. No credit card."

## Common Pitfalls
- Agitation that feels manipulative (keep it truthful, not fear-mongering)
- Problem too generic (specificity creates recognition)
- Solution introduced too early (let the agitation build — the relief feels better)
