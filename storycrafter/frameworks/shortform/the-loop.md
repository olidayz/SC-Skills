# The Loop

## What It Is
A story designed so the ending connects back to the beginning, creating a seamless loop that rewards rewatching. TikTok's algorithm directly rewards rewatch rate — if people watch twice, the video gets pushed. The Loop exploits this by making the second watch more meaningful than the first.

## How It Works

1. **Plant something in the opening** that only makes sense after the ending
2. **Tell the story** — the audience doesn't notice the plant on first watch
3. **End where you began** — but now the opening means something different
4. **The viewer loops** — "Wait, did I see what I think I saw?" → watches again

## Structure

- **Frame 1 (The Plant):** Show something that seems normal but contains a hidden meaning. Could be a visual detail, a word, a background element.
- **Body (The Journey):** Tell your story normally. Build to the ending.
- **Final Frame (The Trigger):** Reveal something that recontextualizes the plant. The audience needs to go back.
- **Seamless Transition:** The final frame should visually or tonally connect to the first frame, so the loop feels natural.

## Types of Loops

### The Hidden Detail Loop
Something visible in the first shot only becomes significant after the reveal.
- Example: A photo on the wall in the background of shot 1 — the ending reveals who's in the photo and why it matters.

### The Meaning-Shift Loop
A word, phrase, or image means one thing at the start and another after the ending.
- Example: "I never saw it coming" — first watch: casual. After ending: ominous.

### The Continuous Loop
The ending literally flows into the beginning — infinite playback.
- Example: Character walks through a door at the end, walks through the same door at the beginning. The loop IS the experience.

### The "Wait, What?" Loop
The ending drops information that makes you question everything you just watched.
- Example: A love story that ends with a reveal that changes who you thought the characters were.

## When to Use It
- **Best for:** TikTok, Reels, any platform where rewatch = algorithmic boost
- **Tone fit:** Mystery, twist, mind-bending, satisfying, clever
- **Format fit:** 15-60s shorts (ideal), music videos, experimental
- **Not ideal for:** Ads with a CTA (you want them to click, not rewatch), educational content

## Example
**A 30s short:**
- Open: A woman packs a suitcase. She pauses, holds a scarf, smiles sadly. Puts it in. (5s)
- Middle: She drives to the airport. Checks in. Boards. Sits. (15s)
- End: Flight attendant: "Welcome back, Mrs. Torres." She looks out the window — we see the same house from the opening. She's going home. (8s)
- The scarf was already home. She was coming back, not leaving. Rewatch: her "sad smile" is actually relief. (Loop triggers)

## Common Pitfalls
- Plant too obvious (audience catches it first watch — no reason to loop)
- Plant too hidden (audience doesn't notice even on rewatch — loop doesn't trigger)
- Ending doesn't actually recontextualize (the loop is just "oh, it repeated" — no new meaning)
