# The Story Spine

## What It Is
Kenn Adams' improv framework, adopted by Pixar. A fill-in-the-blank story structure that forces narrative logic. Deceptively simple — if your story can't fill this template, it probably has structural problems.

## The Template

- **Once upon a time...** (the world and protagonist)
- **Every day...** (the routine, the status quo)
- **But one day...** (the inciting incident)
- **Because of that...** (consequence 1)
- **Because of that...** (consequence 2)
- **Because of that...** (consequence 3 — chain of cause and effect)
- **Until finally...** (the climax — the chain breaks or resolves)
- **And ever since then...** (the new normal — transformation)

## Why "Because of That" Matters
The chain of "because of that" is the spine of causality. Each event causes the next. If you can replace "because of that" with "and then," your story has no causal logic — it's just a sequence of events. Causality = story. Sequence = anecdote.

## When to Use It
- **Best for:** Testing any story's structural integrity, quick story development, pitching
- **Tone fit:** Universal — works for comedy, drama, anything
- **Format fit:** Perfect for shorts (it's already compressed), great for ads, useful as a first-pass for features before expanding with other frameworks
- **Secret weapon:** Use this to test an existing outline. If you can't fill in the spine, the story has holes.

## Example
**Up (Pixar):**
- Once upon a time, there was an old man named Carl who lived alone in his house.
- Every day, he sat in his chair and remembered his wife Ellie.
- But one day, developers threatened to demolish his house.
- Because of that, Carl tied thousands of balloons to his house and flew to South America.
- Because of that, he discovered a boy scout named Russell was accidentally on board.
- Because of that, they faced jungle dangers and met Carl's childhood hero — who turned out to be the villain.
- Until finally, Carl chose to save Russell over preserving Ellie's memory.
- And ever since then, Carl became the adventurer Ellie always wanted him to be — with a new family.

## For Ads (30s)
Compressed spine:
- Every day... (2s — the problem/routine)
- But one day... (5s — discovery of product/solution)
- Because of that... (15s — transformation)
- And ever since then... (8s — new life + brand)

## Common Pitfalls
- "And then" instead of "because of that" — causality is everything
- Too many "because of that" beats — 3 is ideal, more gets unwieldy
- Weak "until finally" — the climax should feel inevitable given the chain
